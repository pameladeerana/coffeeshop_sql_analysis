# Coffee Shop Sales Simple SQL Analysis – Davao City
Analyzing coffee shop sales to find revenue leaks.

## Business Questions
1. What is the total revenue?
2. Which product generates the most revenue?
3. Which day is the slowest? (potential revenue leak)
4. What payment method is most used?
5. What are the peak hours?

## Files
- `coffee_schema.sql` - table structure
- `coffee_data.csv` - sample 10 rows (real dataset would be 1000+)
- `coffee_queries.sql` - 5 business queries with explanations

## Key Insight (from this sample)
- Latte is top revenue product, Tuesdays show lowest sales, mornings 8-10am are peak.
- Recommendation: Run Tuesday promo to fix slow day.
